Job Title: Junior Frontend Developer

Job Description:
We are seeking a motivated Junior Frontend Developer to join our team. The ideal candidate should have a strong understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and be familiar with React.js. Responsibilities include building responsive web interfaces, collaborating with designers and backend developers, and maintaining code quality. Good communication skills and a willingness to learn are essential.

Requirements:

Proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Experience with React.js
Basic understanding of REST APIs
Ability to work in a team environment
Strong problem-solving skills
Preferred:

Experience with version control (Git)
Familiarity with UI/UX principles
